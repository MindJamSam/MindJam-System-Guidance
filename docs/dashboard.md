description: How to use the Salesforce dashboards to check your sessions and billing totals.
steps:
  - title: Opening the Dashboard
    body: |
      The dashboard replaces the timesheet view. It is where you can check useful information about your sessions and billing.

      To open it from the Community Site, go to **Analytics** and click **Dashboards** from the drop-down menu.
    media:
      - src: assets/images/dashboard/dashboard-nav.png
        alt: Salesforce navigation bar showing Analytics open with Dashboards in the drop-down menu

  - title: Daily Totals
    body: |
      **Daily Totals** shows the sessions you have for the current day.

      The table shows the appointment number, mentee name, status, start time, and session date. You can click the **appointment number** to open the Service Appointment.

      The chart on the right shows the status split for the day. If sessions are cancelled or marked with a different status, the chart will update to reflect that.

      If your sessions do not appear straight away, click the **refresh** button on the dashboard component.
    media:
      - src: assets/images/dashboard/daily-totals.png
        alt: Daily Totals dashboard showing today's mentor sessions and a pie chart of session statuses

  - title: Monthly Totals
    body: |
      **Monthly Totals** shows how much you have made in the current month.

      The left-hand component lists the sessions included for that month and the amount linked to each one.

      The right-hand component shows the total amount for the month. If there is no data yet, Salesforce may show an empty chart or a zero total.
    media:
      - src: assets/images/dashboard/monthly-totals.png
        alt: Monthly Totals dashboard showing monthly mentor bills and a total amount component

  - title: Yearly Totals
    body: |
      **Yearly Totals** shows your billing totals grouped by month.

      The left column shows the bill start month. The middle column shows the total amount for that month. The right column shows the record count, which is the number of sessions included in that total.

      In the example above, the mentor has made **£60** from **2 sessions**.

      Months with no sessions will not appear because there is no total to show.
    media:
      - src: assets/images/dashboard/yearly-totals.png
        alt: Yearly Totals dashboard showing monthly billing totals and record count

  - title: Tax Year Totals
    body: |
      **Yearly Totals (April - April)** shows the same kind of information from a tax-year perspective.

      Use this when you want to check totals across the April-to-April tax year rather than by the standard calendar year.
    media:
      - src: assets/images/dashboard/yearly-totals-april.png
        alt: Yearly Totals April to April dashboard showing tax-year billing totals

  - title: Customising the Dashboard
    body: |
      Dashboards can be customised. We can add or remove information, and we can create tables or graphs if there is something useful you would like to see.

      If there is something that would help you, contact Sam by email or Discord and we can discuss whether it can be added.
