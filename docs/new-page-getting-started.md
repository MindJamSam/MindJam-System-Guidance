title: Before You Begin
section: getting-started
description: A heads-up on access, opting in, and what is not live yet.
steps:
  - title: Access to the Site
    body: |
      You will not be able to log into the Community Site just yet. The **invite emails** have not gone out. This guide is here so you can start getting familiar with the **processes**, **what is changing**, and **what the new site looks like**.

      The **videos** throughout each page walk you through the **styling of the system** and what everything means, so you can build a picture of it in advance.
  - title: When You Move Over
    body: |
      We are leaving things **open for now**. If you would rather not move to the new system straight away, **you do not have to**.

      However, until you switch:

      - You **will not be assigned any new mentees**
      - You are still **responsible for your own timekeeping**
  - title: Moving Away From Google
    body: |
      From **2027**, unless there are major problems with the new system, we will be **moving away from the current Google Calendar and Google Sheets** setup entirely.
  - title: Not Live Yet
    body: |
      A couple of things are not launching straight away.

      **Group Sessions** will not be live from the start. We want to give that service a bit more time before it goes live.

      We may also be looking at **introducing Clubs** to the system at the same time.
  - title: Get to Know Funding Types
    body: |
      If you have not done so already, take a little time to **get familiar with the funding type** of each of your mentees. On the new system you will need to know the **funding status of every mentee** you work with, so it is worth starting to build that picture now.

      No pressure to have it all memorised straight away. Just have a look through when you get a moment, so it feels familiar by the time you switch over.
