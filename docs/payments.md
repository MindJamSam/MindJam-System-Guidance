description: How payments are handled and why sessions need to be wrapped up on time.
steps:
  - title: How Payments Work
    body: |
      Mentors no longer need to generate an invoice and share it with Dan.

      Payments are now handled automatically at the end of each month, based on the sessions recorded in Salesforce.

  - title: Wrap Sessions Up On Time
    body: |
      To make sure payment information is correct, sessions need to be wrapped up before **11:59 PM on the final day of each month**.

      You should wrap up each session at the end of the session rather than leaving sessions unwrapped until the end of the month. This keeps your records accurate and avoids a last-minute rush.

  - title: If You Cannot Wrap Them Up
    body: |
      We understand that there may be times when you cannot wrap sessions up before the deadline, for example if you are away.

      If this happens, let **Sam** or a member of **Senior Leadership** know as soon as possible. We can log in to your account and wrap the sessions up for you.

      You will need to tell us what the status of each session was, such as **Attended**, **Did Not Attend**, **Late Notice Cancellation**, or **Cancelled**. This helps make sure the session is recorded correctly.

  - title: Why This Matters
    body: |
      Wrapped-up sessions are used for payment.

      If a session is not wrapped up in time, it may not be included correctly when payments are processed. Keeping sessions wrapped up as you go gives the best chance of everything being paid correctly at the end of the month.
