---
title: How to Navigate the Community Site
section: getting-started
description: >-
  A guide to the main navigation areas in the Salesforce Community Site and what each section is for. When you log in to the Community Site, you will see a navigation bar at the top of the page. This is how you move around the system. Use the navigation bar at the top of the Community Site to move between the main areas: Appointment Management, Support, My Account, Analytics, Profile. This should help you find what you need more quickly.
