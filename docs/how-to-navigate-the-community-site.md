steps:
  - title: Overview
    body: |
      When you log in to the Community Site, you will see a navigation bar at the top of the page. This is how you move around the system.
  - title: Appointment Management
    body: |
      This section is for anything related to sessions, mentees, and availability.

      - [**My Mentees**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/assigned-mentors) — A list of your mentees.
      - [**My Sessions**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/my-appointments) — A list of your scheduled sessions. You can change the list view to show things like today's sessions or this week's sessions.
      - [**Manage Time Slots**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/operating-hours) — This is where you add your availability. For example, if you are free from 9:00 to 10:00 on a Monday, you can add that time slot here.
      - [**Absences**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/absences) — This section is optional. You can use it to record time away, such as holidays. **It does not email parents or carers.**
      - [**Calendar View**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/calendar) — This shows your session information in a calendar format instead of a list.
      - [**Group Sessions**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/session-group/Session_Group__c/00BJ9000003O0cYMAS) — If you run group sessions, you can view them here. These are kept separate from one-to-one sessions.
  - title: Support
    body: |
      - [**Support Tickets**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/support-home) — Use this to log a support ticket if you need help from a member of the team. This can be easier than using Discord or email.
      - [**Knowledge Base**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/knowledge) — This will replace the Wiki. You will be able to find guidance, instructions, and other useful information here.
  - title: My Account
    body: |
      - [**My Account**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/comm-my-account) — This section is where you can update your account information, such as your mobile number or personal email address.
      - **My Invoices** — This shows the invoices you create that are sent to the finance team. It is mainly there for record-keeping.
  - title: Analytics
    body: |
      - **Dashboard** — Shows useful information such as how much you have made in a month and how many sessions you have. This area can be customised, so if there is information you would find useful, you can suggest it to senior leadership.
  - title: Profile
    body: |
      - **Profile** — Another place where you can update your account details and profile picture. This is only visible to you and senior leadership.
  - title: Extra Links on the Home Page
    body: |
      - **Team Meeting Log** — Contains previous team meeting emails. You can use it to go back and check old messages if needed.
      - **Available Mentees** — Opens a separate Google Doc showing mentees who may be more difficult to assign. For example, they may have very specific game interests, particular equipment needs, or a need for a specific skill or match. It is worth checking this regularly if you are looking for additional mentees.
    media:
      - src: assets/images/navigation/buttons.png
        alt: Extra links shown on the Community Site home page
  - title: Key Point
    body: |
      Use the navigation bar at the top of the Community Site to move between the main areas: Appointment Management, Support, My Account, Analytics, Profile. This should help you find what you need more quickly.
  - title: Have a Look Around
    body: |
      Now that you know where everything is, take a few minutes to explore the Community Site on your own. Try these small tasks to get comfortable with the layout:

      - Find your mentee list under Appointment Management
      - Open the Knowledge Base and browse what is available
      - Check your My Account section to make sure your details are correct

      You do not need to change anything yet. The goal is simply to get familiar with where things are.
