title: Finding Your Mentees and Sessions
section: finding-your-mentees-and-sessions
description: Where to find your caseload, bookings, and appointment list on the Community Site.
steps:
  - title: Overview
    body: |
      There are two things you may need to find regularly in Salesforce: your [mentees](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/assigned-mentors) and your [sessions](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/my-appointments).
  - title: Your Mentees
    body: |
      Your **mentees** are the young people assigned to you. Their profiles contain useful information such as their details, games, equipment, and other background information.
  - title: Your Sessions
    body: |
      Your **sessions** are the appointments and events linked to the work you do with mentees. These are where you will usually check dates and times, access the Service Appointment, and complete wrap ups. Sessions will only appear once you have added calendar events to your calendar.
