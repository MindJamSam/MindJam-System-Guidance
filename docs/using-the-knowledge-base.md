title: Using the Knowledge Base
section: resources
description: How to find guidance articles in the Salesforce Knowledge Base.
steps:
  - title: Overview
    body: |
      The **Knowledge Base** is where Salesforce guidance articles are stored. It is replacing the wiki.

      To open it from the Community Site, go to **Support** and click **Knowledge Base**.

      You can also open it directly here: [**Knowledge Base**](https://mindjam.my.site.com/Portal/s/knowledge).

  - title: Finding the Right Article
    body: |
      Use the list controls to search, sort, or filter the articles.

      If you know the area you need, use the category drop-down or list filters to narrow the results. This helps you find the right guidance more quickly.

      Click an **Article Title** to open the article.

  - title: What an Article Looks Like
    body: |
      When you open an article, you will see the article title at the top and the guidance underneath.

      Some articles are short and simple. Others include sections, tables, links, or numbered guidance.
    media:
      - src: assets/images/knowledge-base/article-example.png
        alt: Example Knowledge Base article showing a guidance page with a title and structured content

  - title: Switching Views
    body: |
      Use the view button <img class="aur-inline-view-icon" src="../assets/images/knowledge-base/switching-views-icon.svg" alt="view button icon"> near the top right of the Knowledge Base list to switch between **List View** and **Kanban View**.

      You can use whichever view feels easiest. The articles are the same in both views.

      **List View** shows the articles in a table. This is useful when you want to see the article title, category, summary, and modified date in one place.

      <img class="aur-step__media-img" src="../assets/images/knowledge-base/list-view.png" alt="Knowledge Base list view showing article titles, categories, summaries, and modified dates" loading="lazy" data-aur-lightbox>

      **Kanban View** is a board view. In this Knowledge Base, it shows the same articles as cards arranged into columns.

      The columns help group similar articles together, so you can scan a category and pick the article you need. It is not a different Knowledge Base. It is just another way to view the same information.

      <img class="aur-step__media-img" src="../assets/images/knowledge-base/kanban-view.png" alt="Knowledge Base Kanban view showing articles as cards grouped in columns" loading="lazy" data-aur-lightbox>

  - title: Key Point
    body: |
      Use the **Knowledge Base** when you need written guidance or a process article. If you cannot find the answer there, use the [**Support Ticket**](../how-to-log-a-support-ticket/) section to raise a question.
