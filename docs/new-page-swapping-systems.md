steps:
  - title: Before You Switch
    body: |
      Before you make the switch over to Salesforce, make sure you have completed the **three key tasks** from the **Getting Started** section.
  - title: Your Pre-Switch Checklist
    body: |
      - **Check your mentee list** and confirm everyone is showing
      - Update your **games and equipment**
      - Set your **availability (Operating Hours)**
  - title: Pick Your Date
    body: |
      Once all of that is done and you feel **confident to move over**, pick a **date to switch**. It can be at the **start of the month** or **halfway through**. It genuinely does not matter.

      What does matter is that **all of your mentees are showing in the system** before you start using it for sessions. If any are missing, **do not worry**. We will explain **exactly what to do** on the following pages.
