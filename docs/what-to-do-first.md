title: What to Do First
section: what-to-do-first
description: The first setup steps to complete after getting familiar with the Community Site.
steps:
  - title: Overview
    body: |
      Once you have familiarised yourself with the **Community Site**, there are three things we recommend doing first to complete your setup.
  - title: First Three Tasks
    body: |
      - Update your **game preferences and equipment**
      - Set your **availability (Operating Hours)**
      - **Check your mentee list**
  - title: Why This Matters
    body: |
      Updating your **Games, Equipment, and Operating Hours** tells Salesforce what you can support and when you are available to work. This data allows the assigning team to match you with the best-fit mentees based on shared skills, tech, and capacity. Even if you aren't looking to take on new mentees right now, completing these steps is still highly recommended to ensure your profile is accurate and up to date.

      Before you start using the system, it's also important to **check your assigned mentee list**. You won't be able to set up sessions with anyone who isn't on it. If someone's missing, it's usually down to timing: there was a cut-off when the data was transferred over, plus a short buffer window where the assigning team may have moved mentees around. Flag anything that looks off so we can sort it.
