description: The first setup steps to complete on the Community Site.
steps:
  - title: Overview
    body: |
      There are **four things** we recommend doing first to **complete your setup**.
  - title: First Four Tasks
    body: |
      - Familiarise yourself with the **site and navigating it** (you can do this any time from when you get the confirm login email)
      - **Check your mentee list**
      - Update your **game preferences and equipment**
      - Set your **availability (Operating Hours)**
  - title: Why This Matters
    body: |
      Before you start using the system, it's important to **check your assigned mentee list**. You **won't be able to set up sessions** with anyone who isn't on it. If someone's missing, it's usually down to timing: there was a cut-off when the data was transferred over, plus a short buffer window where the assigning team may have moved mentees around. Flag anything that looks off so we can sort it.

      Once your list is right, updating your **Games, Equipment, and Operating Hours** tells Salesforce what you can support and when you are available to work. This data allows the assigning team to match you with the best-fit mentees based on shared skills, tech, and capacity. Even if you aren't looking to take on new mentees right now, completing these steps is still highly recommended to ensure your profile is accurate and up to date.
  - title: Don't Worry
    body: |
      We will **cover all of these sections shortly**. No need to figure any of it out from here.
