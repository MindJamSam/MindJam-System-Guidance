Need Help

Where to go when you need help — support tickets for system questions, senior leadership for personal questions, and the safeguarding form for safeguarding concerns.

Which channel should I use?

  • System or process questions — raise a support ticket. Tickets give a clear record and route to the right team (Finance, Provision, SEND, General Advice).
  • Training guide questions, or anything personal — contact a member of senior leadership directly using the details below.
  • Safeguarding concerns — use the safeguarding form. Do not use tickets or email for safeguarding.
