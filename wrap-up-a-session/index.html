Wrap Up a Session

How to complete session notes, close out a mentoring session, cancel a session, or handle a late notice cancellation. Also covers attendance, invoicing, and payment.

  1. 1

    Overview

    Wrapping up a session confirms what happened and updates the session record for attendance, invoicing, and payment.

  2. 2

    How to Wrap Up a Session

    Open the Service Appointment. You can reach one from the homepage, calendar, or dashboard — look for a hyperlink that starts with SA. Then click Wrap Up Session in the top right corner.

  3. 3

    Funding Type

    Next, choose whether the session is:

    • Funded
    • Self-funded

    If you are not sure, you can check with Finance or Senior Leadership, or log a ticket with Finance. In many cases, there will only be one option. If there is more than one, choose the one that matches the arrangement for that mentee.

  4. 4

    Why This Matters

    Wrapping up a session is important because it helps the system confirm what happened, send automatic attendance emails, and support invoicing and payment processing.

    If a mentor does not wrap up a session, they will not be paid for that session. Invoices are automatically generated at the end of each month, so please make sure sessions are wrapped up before the final day of each month. If you are unable to wrap up any sessions for any reason, let a member of Senior Leadership know the status of each session (Attended, Cancelled, and so on) and they will wrap your sessions up for you.

  5. 5

    After You Submit

    Once everything has been completed, the Service Appointment will update and the session record will be complete. This is what the system uses for attendance, invoicing, and payment.

  7. 7

    If Somethign is Missing

    Occasionally, the system may stop you from finishing the wrap up because funding information is missing. In that case:

    • Click Wrap Up Session
    • Select the funding type
    • Click Next
    • On the next screen there will be no funding options — click Next again to continue
    • Use the Admin Details box to explain the issue briefly

    You can copy one of these standard messages into the Admin Details box, depending on the funding type. Finance will then find the missing information, add it to the mentee's account, and wrap up that session for you.

  8. 8

    Why Data Might Be Missing

    There are a couple of common reasons the funding information might not be there.

    Due to the recent data move, the funding team may not have added it to the mentee's profile yet.

    Or, if a mentee is usually funded but a parent has decided to self-fund a few sessions during a holiday, the parent's self-funding contact may not have been added.
